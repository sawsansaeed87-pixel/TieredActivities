Proper maths, when you need it. Put LaTeX between dollar signs — $\frac{3}{4}$ gives a real stacked fraction, $\sqrt{x+1}$ a proper radical. Use $$…$$ to centre it on its own line. Everything renders live in the preview under each box, so you can check before you send.

Pictures. Use the button, drag a file onto the box, or just paste with Ctrl+V. Images are shrunk automatically, but they still make the link longer — watch the meter in step 06. One or two diagrams is comfortable; six photos is not.
